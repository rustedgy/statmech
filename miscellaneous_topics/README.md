# Miscellaneous Topics

This section contains original supplementary chapters on concepts in statistical mechanics. These are independent study resources, not transcripts of any particular lecture series.

## Chapters

- [The concept of temperature](temperature_from_statistical_mechanics.md) — entropy, energy exchange, the canonical distribution, and negative temperatures.
- [Maxwell–Boltzmann statistics](maxwell_boltzmann_statistics.md) — derive the classical occupation distribution from state counting and entropy maximization.
- [Bose–Einstein statistics](bose_einstein_statistics_derivation.md) — derive the occupation distribution from bosonic state counting and entropy maximization.
- [Fermi–Dirac statistics](fermi_dirac_statistics_derivation.md) — derive the occupation distribution from fermionic state counting and entropy maximization.
- [Equipartition of energy](equipartition_of_energy.md) — the classical quadratic theorem and the general power-law result for non-quadratic energy terms.
- [Newton's law of cooling from statistical mechanics](newtons_law_of_cooling_statmech.md) — derive exponential cooling as near-equilibrium energy relaxation, using detailed balance, a master equation, and linear response.
