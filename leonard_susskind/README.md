# Statistical Mechanics — Lecture Notes (Leonard Susskind)

## Purpose

This folder contains study notes prepared to accompany selected lectures in a YouTube statistical mechanics lecture series. The notes are intended as a convenient reference for revision: they organize the main ideas, definitions, equations, and reasoning into a lecture-notes format.

## Sources and credit

The notes are based on the original [Statistical Mechanics by Leonard Susskind at Stanford](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLpGHT1n4-mAsJ123W3fjPzvlDHOvIhHA0).

**Credit for note preparation:** ChatGPT (OpenAI) has been used to generate these notes from the linked lecture and the transcript provided. The notes have been updated here as generated. The notes may contain omissions or errors; compare them with the original source when accuracy matters.

- [Lecture 1 notes](statistical_mechanics_lecture_1_notes.md) — topic-based study notes on microstates, macrostates, multiplicity, probability, entropy, and equilibrium.
- [Lecture 2 — topic-based notes](statistical_mechanics_lecture_2_notes.md) — a topic-organized overview of the second lecture.
- [Lecture 2 — transcript-based notes](statistical_mechanics_lecture_2_transcript_based_notes.md) — a separate version based on the supplied transcript, with more transcript-specific coverage of temperature, energy units, Boltzmann's constant, equilibrium distributions, and heat transfer.

**Choosing a Lecture 2 version:** Start with the topic-based notes for a concise conceptual overview. Use the transcript-based version for the more detailed account grounded in the supplied transcript. These are alternative treatments of the same lecture, not two consecutive lectures.
